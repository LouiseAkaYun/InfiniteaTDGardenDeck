Operations subpage / proposed pilot SOP

Model K cleaning & closing workflow.

A concise daily and weekly workflow for the proposed TD Garden pilot. The final SOP, chemistry, hold times and recordkeeping must be approved with the TD Garden food-safety team before launch.

Planning note: This page translates the proposed machine workflow into an operator checklist. It is not a substitute for the manufacturer manual, local health-code requirements or TD Garden's approved sanitation program.

Daily close

1
Stop sales and secure the stationRemove finished cups from the premade zone and record any discarded product.
2
Connect the cleaning setupInsert and connect the designated cleaning tubes according to the manufacturer procedure.
3
Run the automatic cleaning cycleUse water and the approved cleaning agent at the validated concentration and cycle time.
4
Wipe food-contact surroundingsClean and sanitize the dispensing bay, cup platform, touchscreen, handles and nearby work surface.
5
Inspect and sign offConfirm no visible residue, leaks or damaged seals; complete the closing log.

Weekly / 1–2×

1
Remove ingredient containersWash, rinse and sanitize removable containers and lids using the approved warewashing process.
2
Inspect tubing and sealsCheck for residue, wear, kinks, loose connections or off-odors; replace components when required.
3
Deep-clean adjacent equipmentClean shaker holders, sealing-machine contact areas, ice-prep tools and storage surfaces.
4
Reassemble and verifyReconnect components, run a rinse / verification cycle and confirm correct dispense behavior.
5
Review the logSupervisor verifies completion and escalates any maintenance or food-safety concern.

Opening check

1
Visual inspectionConfirm the station is clean, dry, assembled and free of overnight leakage.
2
Temperature and hold-time checkVerify refrigerated storage and ingredient hold times against the approved pilot plan.
3
Test dispenseRun the required opening rinse / test cup and check portion, appearance and flavor.
4
Prepare toolsStage sanitized shaker cups, serving cups, ice scoop, sealing film and waste container.

Pilot measurements

1
Daily cleaning minutesTrack employee touch time separately from the automatic cycle.
2
Completion rateDocument checklist completion and corrective actions by event.
3
Waste and hold-time lossesRecord discarded ingredients and finished drinks to refine batch planning.
4
Maintenance incidentsLog leaks, clogs, seal issues or operator errors and response time.
