Pilot subpage / responsibility checklist

Infinitea Launch Checklist

A turnkey pilot structure: Mascon supplies the equipment, menu, training, ingredient program and support; TD Garden supplies the operating location, utilities, event-day team and venue systems.

What Mascon Provides

3 Model K machinesLeased, delivered and installed for automated dosing and dispensing.
Ingredients & prep kitIngredient supply program plus tea brewer, fast-cook boba cooker and operating containers.
Arena menu & recipesA focused, high-throughput milk-tea and fruit-tea menu for the pilot.
Crew trainingOn-site training for TD Garden's event-day team before launch.
On-site supervisorA Mascon representative on site to monitor operation and resolve pilot issues.
Monitoring & supportTelemetry and support for cup output, ingredient consumption and machine status.

What TD Garden Needs to Provide

Floor space & clearanceA high-traffic concourse location with an estimated operating footprint of approximately 5 sq ft for the full stand setup.
Utilities & ice makerPower, water and drainage, plus an ice maker and chilled storage where required; final requirements confirmed during the site walk.
NetworkWi-Fi or other approved connectivity for ordering, telemetry and operational support.
Event-day staffVenue employees operate the stand and complete routine upkeep; Mascon trains and supports the team.
POS / paymentOrdering and payment through TD Garden's approved system, with agreed sales and inventory data sharing.
Placement & accessEvent-day access for delivery, service and the Mascon pilot representative.
At a glance: Mascon supplies the specialty-drink program end to end; TD Garden supplies the space, utilities, event-day staff, ice capability, network and POS.
Pilot responsibilities remain subject to site validation and the signed pilot agreement.
