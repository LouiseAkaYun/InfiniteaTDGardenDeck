Opportunity 01

A premium alcohol-free occasion

Young-adult drinking is declining, but the desire for a social, premium and customizable beverage experience remains. Boba occupies a different price and experience tier than fountain soda while remaining accessible to guests of every age.

1
Broader eligibilityUnder-21 guests and non-drinkers can purchase the same premium product.
2
High perceived valueCustomization, texture, sealed presentation and visual appeal support a specialty-drink price point.
3
Incremental spendThe pilot tests whether boba captures purchases that would otherwise be water, soda or no drink.
Opportunity 02

Specialty drinks without a traditional tea bar

The automated process standardizes recipes and separates machine time from employee time. The employee starts the dispense cycle, uses the automated wait time for cup preparation, then completes shaking, pouring, sealing and restocking.

1
Recipe controlSelections run through the touchscreen rather than manual measuring at the stand.
2
Parallel workAutomated dispense and shake time can overlap with ice preparation and other light tasks.
3
Premade bufferThe guest-facing cashier retrieves completed drinks instead of waiting for a fresh production cycle.
Opportunity 03

Protect the short selling window

Venue demand arrives in waves. The operating model builds an appropriate premade buffer before doors or intermission, then uses continuous replenishment to protect availability while the cashier focuses on fast handoff.

1
ConcertsProduce ahead of the preshow arrival wave, then replenish against actual sales.
2
GamesUse pregame production plus targeted replenishment before intermission windows.
3
Pilot measurementTrack cups per five-minute interval to size the buffer and unit count by event type.
