Why 01 / Category growth

Why Boba Tea Grows

Boba combines flavor, customization and social appeal in a premium alcohol-free drink that younger guests already recognize.

1
Alcohol use among younger adults is declining A premium zero-proof drink can serve a social occasion without relying on alcohol.
2
It is visual and customizable Milk tea, fruit tea, pearls and other toppings create a product that feels personal and shareable.
3
Variety supports repeat purchase Guests can return for different flavors, sweetness levels and topping combinations.
Why 02 / Venue fit

Why It Belongs to TD Garden

TD Garden already has the event volume and premium hospitality environment. Boba adds a specialty-drink option for guests who are currently underserved by the alcohol-focused beverage mix.

1
It reaches a different guest Under-21 fans, families, designated drivers and guests choosing not to drink can all purchase the same premium product.
2
It adds rather than replaces The concept is intended to generate incremental beverage spend inside stands TD Garden already operates.
3
It fits the event format A premade buffer and rapid cashier handoff support concert preshow and game intermission demand.
Why 03 / Operating fit

Why Infinitea Model K

Model K is designed to add a specialty-drink platform without creating a large traditional boba counter or a labor-heavy manual production line.

1
Compact Its narrow footprint is intended for constrained service lines and existing concession layouts.
2
Mobile Casters allow the unit to be rolled into position and relocated for different event configurations.
3
Built for lean operation Automated dispensing supports an efficient, consistent premade-drink cycle.
